QUESTIONS

Before you register

Are the courses accredited, and will I get a certificate?

It depends on the provider and the course. We will state clearly on each path whether a certificate is offered and who issues it. Nothing is promised before it is confirmed.

Who teaches the courses?

The core content comes from established course providers. AKH adds live coaching, practical projects and support from people who build digital products every day.

Do I need experience?

No. Every path has a starting point for complete beginners, and a faster route if you already have some skills.

Can my employer pay for it?

Yes. Many learners are funded by their employer. We can provide a clear invoice and a short summary of the learning outcomes.

When do the paths open?

The paths are in development. Register your interest and we will tell you first, with early-access pricing for the first cohort.

Tell us who you are and what you want to learn. You’ll hear first when your path opens, at an early-access price.
