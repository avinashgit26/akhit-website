Goddiva named Retailer Tech Innovator of the Year at the Drapers Awards 2026.

One night after its Boutique Star Awards win, Goddiva took home a second tech award. Co-founders Kuganeswaran Thangarajah and Mina Melikova collected it in London.

The night at a glance · Drapers Awards 2026 · London

  • Retailer Tech Innovator of the Year 2026: Goddiva won
  • Collected by co-founders Kuganeswaran Thangarajah and Mina Melikova
  • Two tech awards in one week

From shortlist to winner

Shortlisted in 2025. Winner in 2026.

In 2025, Goddiva was shortlisted five times at the Drapers Awards, including Tech Innovator of the Year. This year it won the tech category.

Drapers has covered British fashion retail for generations, so this is recognition from the trade itself. Goddiva’s aim is simple: use technology to make fashion retail more personal, seamless and fun.
