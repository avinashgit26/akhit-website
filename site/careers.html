How we work together

Clear goals, weekly demos, tidy code.

Every project has one owner, a fixed scope and a weekly rhythm, so you always know what matters this week and how it will be judged.

  • Plan on Monday. Agree the week’s goals with your lead and the client.
  • Build and review. Small pull requests, reviewed the same day where we can.
  • Demo on Friday. Show working software to the client and hear their feedback first hand.
  • Modern tools. React, Laravel, Shopify and AI services, chosen for each project rather than by habit.

About the role

We are recruiting on behalf of an established, award-winning London fashion brand. You’ll be the person customers trust for answers, and you’ll help the team hit its service goals.

What you’ll do
  • Understand what each customer needs and make sure they leave satisfied
  • Answer questions about products, pricing and availability, and explain the value of the range
  • Help the team reach its service and sales goals by spotting leads when they come up
  • Follow the brand’s communication guidelines and procedures with little supervision
What you’ll need
  • Proven experience in a customer service role
  • Strong communication skills, including active listening
  • Problem solving, with the judgement to resolve or tactfully escalate issues
  • The ability to multitask, manage your time and set priorities
  • Comfortable working on your own and as part of a team
  • Working knowledge of Microsoft Office
Nice to have
  • Experience with e-commerce back-office systems
What you get
  • Hybrid working
  • Possible UK sponsorship through the client, an A-rated licensed sponsor (terms and conditions apply)
