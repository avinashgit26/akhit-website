How a free AI fit session works

Illustrative example: invoice processing
1

You bring a workflow

We list every step the way your team really does it.

  • 1
    Invoice arrives by emailA PDF or email lands in the shared inbox.
  • 2
    Read and enter the dataSomeone types totals and dates into the system.
  • 3
    Match to purchase orderChecked by hand against the order.
  • 4
    Approve the paymentA manager signs off.
  • 5
    Pay and filePayment goes out and the invoice is filed.
2

We run five checks

Before any AI is suggested.

  • ✓
    Is the process stable and repeatable?Yes. Same steps every time.
  • −
    Do simple rules fall short?Partly. Invoice layouts vary.
  • ✓
    Is the data usable?Yes. Past invoices to learn from.
  • ✓
    What if it gets one wrong?Low risk. A person checks the totals.
  • ✓
    Can we measure the result?Yes. Time and errors per invoice.
3

We size the AI share

Where AI helps, and what stays simple.

  • 1
    Invoice arrives by emailSimple rule
  • 2
    Read and enter the dataAI step
  • 3
    Match to purchase orderAI + review
  • 4
    Approve the paymentPerson
  • 5
    Pay and fileSimple rule
2 of 5steps use AI. About 40% of this workflow, and only one of them runs without review.
  • Start with step 2 only
  • Pilot first: small
  • Rest stays simple

Verdict: AI belongs in two steps, not the whole process.

Example only. Your own workflow, checks and results will differ.
