Where they started

Every brand sold alone, on its own website.

The client wanted one marketplace where any vendor could sign up and sell, with a back office like Shopify, user-specific roles and the ability to publish products, add stock and update prices in bulk.

What we built

A back office any fashion vendor can pick up.

A one-stop back office where sellers manage products, orders, customers and discounts, with role-based access to each section.

  • Vendor sign-up and subscriptions
  • Products, orders, customers and discounts in one place
  • Role-based access to each section
  • Bulk tools for stock and price updates

Vendors subscribe, upload their products and start selling through one platform.
