Marketplace: many fashion brands, one storefront.

Client: City Goddess, UK fashion group. Brands that used to sell only on their own websites now sell together from one platform.

The problem

Each fashion brand sold only on its own website. The client wanted one marketplace where any vendor could sign up and sell, with a back office like Shopify, user-specific roles and the ability to publish products, add stock and update prices in bulk.

What we built

A one-stop back office where sellers manage products, orders, customers and discounts, with role-based access to each section. Market research and customer profiling shaped an interface any fashion vendor could pick up quickly, and bulk tools take the grind out of updating stock and prices.

The result

Vendors subscribe, upload their products and start selling through one platform. The client has built and kept valuable customer relationships, and revenue has increased.
