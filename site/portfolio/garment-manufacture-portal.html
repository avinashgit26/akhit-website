Garment portal: every order, factory and supplier in one place.

Client: UK fashion manufacturer. Production teams now track every style, order and supplier from desktop or phone.

The problem

Production ran across spreadsheets, emails and calls. The client needed to create styles and spec sheets, raise orders for factories and suppliers, and manage post-production (photoshoots, retouching and pricing) while finding the fastest route to allocate orders to factories.

What we built

A portal that works on desktop and mobile, built around each person on the production line. It covers styles, spec sheets and factory orders; fabric and trim orders that notify the right supplier automatically; and post-production tasks and pricing once garments are made.

The result

Everyone can see pending and completed tasks and act on them straight away. Other manufacturing companies have since asked for the same system for their own production flow.
