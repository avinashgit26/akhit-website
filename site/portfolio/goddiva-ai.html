The problem

Customers had to dig through filters to find the right dress, and the support team spent its day answering the same order, delivery and return questions.

What we built

A chat assistant that works as a personal stylist, shopping guide and support desk in one. Customers describe a style or occasion in plain English and get matching products; it also tracks orders and deliveries and handles returns and refunds. It runs on GPT with retrieval from Goddiva’s own product, order and logistics systems, so answers stay accurate.

The result

Goddiva reports higher customer engagement, more sales through better product discovery, fewer returns thanks to better fit and style suggestions, and less pressure on support through instant self-service.
