## Shared direction

Use the same visual direction for all three: wide 16:9 3D editorial product render, light grey studio background, soft shadows, clean three-quarter view, and forest green, emerald, lime, white and charcoal accents. Keep the main objects fully in frame so the images crop well in the cards.

## Marketplace

Create a premium 3D editorial render for a multi-brand fashion marketplace card. Show a central modern smartphone and compact seller-management tablet, surrounded by a curated mix of fashion products from distinct independent labels: folded clothing, a handbag, shoes and accessories. Suggest bulk stock, pricing and order management with small abstract product tiles, inventory markers and connected parcels. The scene should communicate “many fashion brands, one storefront” and a single back office for sellers. Use a light grey studio background, forest green and emerald materials with restrained lime accents, soft realistic shadows, polished but approachable materials, and a clean three-quarter camera view. Landscape 16:9 composition with generous margins; keep the main objects centered and fully visible. No readable text, logos, brand names, or people.

## Goddiva AI

Create a premium 3D editorial render for a fashion AI shopping-assistant case-study card. Feature a modern smartphone displaying an elegant emerald evening dress as the main product, with subtle surrounding objects suggesting a personal styling conversation: abstract chat bubbles without text, a size-selection tag, a small shopping bag and a simple order-tracking parcel. Make it clear that a customer can describe what they want, find a suitable dress and follow an order. Use a light grey studio background, forest green and emerald with restrained lime accents, soft realistic shadows, polished materials and the same clean three-quarter camera view as a coordinated product-render series. Landscape 16:9 composition with generous margins; keep the objects fully in frame. No readable text, logos, brand names, or people.

## RetailWeave

Create a premium 3D editorial render for an in-house retail operations platform card. Show a central tablet with an abstract, text-free operations dashboard, connected visually to neatly arranged retail products, seller parcels, inventory boxes, a return package and a small finance/order document represented with simple icon-like shapes. The scene should communicate one platform connecting orders, stock, sellers, returns and finance, used by a real retail business. Use a light grey studio background, forest green and emerald with restrained lime accents, subtle charcoal details, soft realistic shadows, polished materials and the same clean three-quarter camera view as a coordinated product-render series. Landscape 16:9 composition with generous margins; keep the objects fully in frame. No readable text, logos, brand names, or people.

## Shared negative prompt

Flat vector illustration, screenshot collage, photorealistic people, busy composition, dark background, harsh shadows, clipped objects, tiny unreadable UI text, gibberish lettering, watermarks, logos, brand marks.
