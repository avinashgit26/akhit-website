Scope creep red flags

  • “Can we just add one more thing?”Every addition has a cost. Put it on the next-phase list first.
  • No single decision-makerFeedback from five people with no final say slows everything down.
  • Success that can’t be measuredIf you can’t say when it’s done, it never is.
  • “We’ll know it when we see it”Agree examples or a prototype before the build starts.
  • A budget agreed before the scopeFix the scope first, then the price, so both stay honest.
