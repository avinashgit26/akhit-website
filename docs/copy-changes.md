# AKH IT website: copy changes for review

Every line below was rewritten to answer at least one of three questions for the visitor:

1. **What result will I get?**
2. **Why AKH over the next agency?**
3. **What will it cost, and how do I find out?**

Prices are hidden for now, so cost is covered by how pricing works: a free 30-minute scoping call, then a fixed quote within 48 hours, and an honest "you don't need custom software" when that's true.

Lines marked **⚑ confirm** are promises or facts the business must be able to keep or evidence. Lines marked **(unchanged)** were left as they are on purpose (stats, Learn placeholders, testimonial quotes).

**Where the build differs from this deck:**

- **Service card link:** says "See what you get →", not "See examples →". Only three services have case studies, so "examples" would over-promise.
- **Checklist:** called the **24-question** checklist, not 20 (the PDF has 8 sections of 3 questions).
- **Home Work cards:** use the qualitative results from the original case studies. The `[metric]` placeholders are only on the case-study pages, so the home page shows no brackets.
- **Contact form:** four fields — name, work email, what you need (dropdown) and budget range (dropdown). Branch phone numbers are left out; the old ones looked like dummy numbers.
- **Goddiva screenshot:** cropped to the top row. The bottom row showed a real customer's email address.

**Also needing confirmation:**

- **Scoper Path A proof line** ⚑ confirm: "order allocation went from days to minutes" is the boss's wording, not something the original case study states.
- **Privacy policy:** a generic template from 2022, ported unchanged. It mentions "www.website.com" and Google DoubleClick ads, which this site doesn't use. It needs a legal review.
